Research-area pastels (up to 7 research areas)

One slot per area (pastel in research.yml, class .a1–.a7). Fill, edge and dot, in that order. Slots 1–3 are the most distinct from one another, so the first three areas never sit close. Ink on a fill stays --text. A separate palette from the spectrum, so an area never shares a colour with a paper type.

Components

  • .navSticky bar: logo, links, contact button; below 780px the links sit behind a Menu button
  • .cta-bannerRecruitment strip, in-grid tile, and Join Us block. On phones the strip is one row (~53px): text beside a smaller Join Us pill that keeps a 44px tap area
  • .section-headerHeading plus optional right-hand link
  • .project-blockResearch area tile with name-seeded graph; the whole tile opens its area on /research/
  • .news-itemDated tile; --wide spans two columns; --link makes the whole card open its entry on /news/
  • .person-cardGraph ring around photo or initials; --compact (photo replaces the ring when present), --director
  • .person-rowRow in a folded list: 28px photo (the original file scaled down) or name-seeded ring, name, muted note; for alumni and former collaborators
  • .foldClosed-by-default <details> list: Alumni (graduate, undergraduate) and Former collaborators
  • .publication-rowVenue, title, authors, links; an optional thumbnail sits under the venue (__figure) and an optional Video button joins the links. The label strip (.pub-labels) closes the main column, level with the thumbnail's bottom. On phones (up to 640px) a row is a compact card: the 96px thumbnail sits beside the venue and title (data-figure rows), authors run full width, and the labels and links share one footer line
  • .pub-labelsLabel strip at the foot of a publication row and of a Research card (_includes/pub-labels.html): the type pill, then one area pill for each research area the paper sits in, none when it sits in no area
  • .pillSmall rounded label. --type: outlined, with the type's dot (colour from publication_types.yml, grey for preprints). --area: the area's pastel fill and edge with dark text (pastel and short name from research.yml; palette in Colour above)
  • .yearPublications year as a native fold: chevron, year, count, and the year's mix of paper types (__types). Only the newest year starts open; year-folds.js opens the year around a link such as #pub-<id> and adds Expand all. On phones the type counts show as coloured dots and numbers on one line; each name stays in the page for screen readers and as a tooltip (__type-label)
  • .pub-filtersPublications filter bar, hidden until year-folds.js runs: one row of search box, Type and Research area menus, then Clear (while filtering) or Expand all (otherwise) in the same slot, and a live result line. State is mirrored to the query string. Below 640px the two menus, Clear and Expand all sit behind one .pub-filters__toggle (Filters, with a count badge), so the controls take one row when closed; opening it shows both menus flat
  • .pub-menuNative <details> dropdown that holds a chip group, so the chips take no room until opened; its summary shows a count of chips that are on. One open at a time; a click outside or Escape closes it
  • .chipPill toggle (aria-pressed): filled when on. At least 44px tall; chips in one group add up, groups narrow each other. A paper-type chip carries that type's dot, and a research-area chip carries its area's dot and, while off, the area's tint: the same colours as the pills on each paper (one per type and per area, from publication_types.yml and research.yml)
  • .medal🏅 after the venue of an Honourable Mention paper, on publication rows and Research cards and links (_includes/medal.html); decorative, since the venue text already names the award
  • .paper-cardThumbnail card for a full paper in a research area (3-up grid; a swipeable, snapping strip on phones, with the next card peeking in): figure, venue, title; the whole card opens that paper's row on /publications/ (#pub-<id>)
  • .paper-linkOne-line link (venue, title) to a paper's row on /publications/; for the area's PWiP, LBW and workshop papers. On phones it flows as text, the venue a small prefix on the title's first line (a few long venues wrap first)
  • .albumOne event on /gallery/ (_includes/gallery-album.html): a native <details> that is also a grid item, under a year heading (newest first) with the other albums of that year (.album-cards, 260px columns, two on phones). Its category is what the chips filter on. Without JS it opens in place, taking the whole row, to show the summary, a Read more link and every photo; with JS pressing it opens the lightbox instead, so the page stays a short list of covers however many albums there are. gallery-index.html draws the chips and the years for both /gallery/ and the specimens below
  • .album__cardThe album's <summary>: its first photo as the cover (4:3), the title, then date, place and photo count. aria-haspopup="dialog", because with JS it opens one
  • .album__gridJustified rows in plain CSS, in an opened card and in the lightbox's contact sheet: each tile is one row tall (220px, 150px in the sheet, 140px and 110px on phones) and grows in proportion to its width over height (--ar, held between 0.6 and 2.4 so a panorama is cropped, not stretched), so every row fills the width and keeps the photos' shape; the last row stays natural. Tab order is reading order. Without JS a tile is a plain link to the large image
  • .slideshowThe opening photo of /gallery/ (_includes/gallery-slideshow.html, gallery-slideshow.js); left out of the specimen below so this page has no autoplaying hero. Every photo of every album is shuffled without replacement (each shows once before any repeats) and crossfades every 6 s over 1.2 s, with a slow 4% drift. A fixed-shape stage (3:2, 4:3 on phones, never taller than 70vh) shows each photo whole over a blurred copy of itself, so portraits keep their heads and nothing below moves. Pressing the photo opens the lightbox on it; the caption names and dates the album and opens its contact sheet. Pause/Play, Previous and Next (44px) sit beside the caption. It holds while the mouse is over it, while a keyboard user is on Previous, Next or the photo, while the tab is hidden, while the lightbox is open and while it is scrolled out of view. With reduced motion it starts paused, fades in 0.3 s and does not drift. Without JS a still of the first album cover stands in
  • .figure-dialogOne native dialog, shared by /publications/ and /gallery/ (_includes/figure-dialog.html, figure.js): the large figure with its description, or a video still that becomes a player on press. An album has two views. Sheet (is-sheet): opened by its card, with the album's facts and summary above a scrolling grid of every photo; a tile opens that photo. Photo (is-grouped): a fixed-height dark stage so the dialog does not jump, the caption, All photos (back to the sheet), Previous and Next (44px), a counter, the arrow keys and a swipe, wrapping at both ends. An album of one photo skips the sheet. On phones it fills the screen. /gallery/#album-<slug> opens an album
  • .valueJoin Us theme: ink line icon with one spectrum accent on a pale tinted disc (drawn in _includes/icon.html)

Rules

Field colour. Warmest at 13:00 Singapore time (yellow, orange), coolest at 01:00 (teal, blue), sliding smoothly along the six spectrum stops. Add ?hour=2 to preview a time.

Field motion. Nodes drift. Near the cursor they gather, link at longer range and shift warmer.

Without the field. No JS: a pre-rendered SVG, warm on the left and cool on the right. Reduced motion: one still frame, no loop. Nothing is written only in the field.

Name graph. Seed = FNV-1a hash of the name. 7 to 11 nodes on a ring, ring edges plus seeded chords, three adjacent spectrum stops walking round the ring. Centre holds a photo or the first two initials.

Text and spectrum. Spectrum stops colour graphics only. Text is --text or --text-muted; the UI accent is ink (--text), with --spec-6 for focus rings.

Paper figures. Optional per paper, never a placeholder: a row without a figure is plain text. The thumbnail is 480 by 300 (16:10, may be a tighter crop than the large image) shown at 168 wide; the large image is at most 1600 wide. Alt text says what the figure shows, not the paper title. Only the thumbnail and Video button open the dialog; the title and Paper button do not. Without JS they are plain links to the image and to YouTube.

Paper videos. Nothing from YouTube loads on page load. A 16:9 poster hosted here stands in until play is pressed, then a youtube-nocookie.com player replaces it. Closing the dialog removes the player. Never commit a video file.

Gallery photos. Albums by event, never a placeholder: a photo is added only inside an album that records the day its people agreed to web use (consent in gallery.yml), and that covers any caption that names someone. An album is named YYYYMMDD-name (its date, 00 for a missing month or day), which is its folder and its #album- anchor; the page orders albums by that name, newest first. Images sit in assets/img/gallery/<album>/ as NN.jpg plus NN-thumb.jpg, at most 1280px on the long side and 350 KB, upright, sRGB and stripped of EXIF and GPS by bin/figure photo. Alt text says what the photo shows. The page is unpublished until the first album exists.

Logo. No plate: the logo is transparent and sits on white. A soft white halo (--halo) behind it keeps field lines out of the mark.
