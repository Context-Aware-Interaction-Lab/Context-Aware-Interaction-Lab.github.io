Rules

Field colour. Warmest at 13:00 Singapore time (yellow, orange), coolest at 01:00 (teal, blue), sliding smoothly along the six spectrum stops. Add ?hour=2 to preview a time.

Field motion. Nodes drift. Near the cursor they gather, link at longer range and shift warmer.

Without the field. No JS: a pre-rendered SVG, warm on the left and cool on the right. Reduced motion: one still frame, no loop. Nothing is written only in the field.

Name graph. Seed = FNV-1a hash of the name. 7 to 11 nodes on a ring, ring edges plus seeded chords, three adjacent spectrum stops walking round the ring. Centre holds a photo or the first two initials.

Text and spectrum. Spectrum stops colour graphics only. Text is --text or --text-muted; the UI accent is ink (--text), with --spec-6 for focus rings.

Paper figures. Optional per paper, never a placeholder: a row without a figure is plain text. The thumbnail is 480 by 300 (16:10, may be a tighter crop than the large image) shown at 168 wide; the large image is at most 1600 wide. Alt text says what the figure shows, not the paper title. Only the thumbnail and Video button open the dialog; the title and Paper button do not. Without JS they are plain links to the image and to YouTube.

Paper videos. Nothing from YouTube loads on page load. A 16:9 poster hosted here stands in until play is pressed, then a youtube-nocookie.com player replaces it. Closing the dialog removes the player. Never commit a video file.

Logo. No plate: the logo is transparent and sits on white. A soft white halo (--halo) behind it keeps field lines out of the mark.
