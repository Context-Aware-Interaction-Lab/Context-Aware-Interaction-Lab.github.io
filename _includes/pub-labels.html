{%- comment -%} item a publication. Prints its label strip: the type pill (the type's dot and short name), then one tinted pastel pill per research area whose `papers` or `more` list the paper's id, in research.yml order (the area's `pastel`, 1-7, is its .aN class). A paper in no area gets no area pill. Also assigns `label_areas` (space-separated slugs, or "other") for the caller: the publication row's data-areas, which assets/js/year-folds.js filters on. One lookup, so the pills and the filter agree. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign label_areas = "" -%} {%- assign type_info = site.data.publication_types | where: "id", include.item.type | first -%}
{{ type_info.short }} {%- if include.item.id -%} {%- for area in site.data.research.areas -%} {%- if area.papers contains include.item.id or area.more contains include.item.id -%} {%- assign label_areas = label_areas | append: " " | append: area.slug -%} {{ area.short }} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endif -%}
